Drop the CH340 driver installer in this folder, e.g.

    CH341SER.EXE
    or CH34x_Install_Windows_v3_4.EXE

You'll find it inside the Multicomp Pro software zip
(https://www.farnell.com/software/4437818.zip -> "driver.zip" / "Serial port chip driver_CH34x...zip"),
or download CH341SER.EXE from WCH: https://www.wch-ic.com/downloads/CH341SER_EXE.html

build.bat bakes whatever .exe is here into PSU Studio.exe, and the
"Install driver" button in the app runs it (with the Windows admin prompt).
If nothing is here, the button opens the WCH download page instead.
